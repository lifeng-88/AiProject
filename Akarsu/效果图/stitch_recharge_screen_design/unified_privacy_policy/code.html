Privacy Policy

This document outlines our commitment to protecting your digital identity and how we process data within our AI ecosystem.

Section 01

Information Collection

Our AI system is designed with a privacy-first architecture. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide a personalized experience. This includes account information such as your email address and basic profile details.

Interaction data, including prompt history and model responses, is processed to improve machine learning performance. However, this data is anonymized and stripped of personally identifiable information (PII) before being used in our training sets.

Section 02

Use of Information

The data we collect serves several core functions within the Digital Curator framework:

  • check_circle Tailoring AI responses to match your unique linguistic style and preferences.
  • check_circle Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity or misuse of our computational resources.
  • check_circle Communicating critical security updates and new feature availability.

Section 03

Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (AES-256) for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Our infrastructure is hosted on secure, distributed cloud environments with physical security measures and 24/7 monitoring.

While no system is entirely impenetrable, we perform regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments to ensure our "Neon Sovereign" security standards are met consistently.

Section 04

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. This excludes trusted third parties who assist us in operating our application, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information strictly confidential.

Section 05

User Rights

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. At any time, you may request access to the data we hold, demand corrections to inaccurate information, or request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data from our servers.
